## 5. What this run does and does not prove

**Proves (each with the artifact that carries it):**

- The disruption was **real, correctly targeted and in-window**: the run's own VMI uid lost and replaced
  (b203235a… → 29dfe33c…), the parent VM persisting, no collateral VM affected, after the full `Done` +
  checkpoint and 89 s before the incremental object (`R01/checkers/injection-validity.json` `.verdict` +
  `.criteria[].status`, 9 exclusions; `R01/observer/watches/{vmi,pods-vm}.jsonl`).
- **CBT recovered across a VMI restart**: checkpoint redefinition succeeded in the restarted launcher,
  the tracker checkpoint was preserved unchanged through the restart, and the next pass was generated
  from the pre-restart full checkpoint (`L01/logs/virt-launcher.log:1304,1308,1309,1364`;
  `R01/observer/watches/tracker.jsonl`).
- The next backup was a **genuine Incremental**, not a Full fallback — explicit `status.type`, distinct
  checkpoint, 17 s vs 55 s, and a restore whose cumulative content matches the mutated guest manifest
  byte-for-byte by SHA-256 (`R01/observer/watches/vmbackup-run.jsonl:13,16`; `L01/run.json`
  `.backups.incrementals[0]`; `L01/logs/restore-verify-pod.log:4-10`; `L01/summary.json`
  `.restore_hashes`).
- The **named failure mode — a silent wrong-content incremental — is affirmatively excluded** by an
  independent restore measurement, which is exactly the spec's decisive test (`SPEC` §5/§7;
  `R01/checkers/evidence-sufficiency.json` `.criteria[1].detail`).
- The **VM was fully recovered before the incremental stage**: new uid Running/Ready 74 s before the
  first incremental object, with the baseline manifest re-validated before mutation
  (`R01/observer/watches/vmi.jsonl:55-59`; `L01/logs/workflow.log:69`).

**Does not prove:**

- **Block-extent-level incrementality** — that the pass-1 image physically holds only blocks changed
  since the checkpoint. No qcow2 allocation-map or dirty-bitmap-extent artifact exists, and
  `L01/summary.json` `.scope` states the summary "does not independently inspect qcow2 allocation
  maps". The residual's only alternative shape (a self-contained full image mislabelled `Incremental`)
  would keep content correct and is contradicted by `status.type`, by
  `L01/logs/virt-launcher.log:1364` and by the 17 s vs 55 s durations — but it is not *proven* absent
  (`R01/checkers/evidence-sufficiency.json` `.missing_evidence[0]`; `R01/observer/finding.md`).
- **Anything below the KubeVirt API/log layer** for the checkpoint itself: QEMU/libvirt state was never
  inspected (no `oc exec` — H4), so the redefinition claim rests on the controller/launcher logs plus the
  API-visible checkpoint names.
- **The evaluator's verdict is not a spec verdict.** It models **none** of §5 and returned `INVALID` on
  inputs that this scenario does not produce by design (HV1/H2); the run verdict rests on the two
  independent checkers, as the team contract requires.
- **Generality.** One run, one OS profile (rhel9), one storage variant class, one restart position
  (between a full and its first incremental). Nothing here bounds behaviour for other checkpoint ages, a
  restart mid-incremental, a multi-pass chain, or a different backend, and the Full-fallback branch was
  not exercised (the incremental branch was — the experiment's subject is a single binary outcome,
  `SPEC` §7).
